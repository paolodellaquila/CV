Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how personal data is processed when you visit https://paolodellaquila.github.io/CV/ (the “Site”), pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

1. Data controller

Francesco Paolo Dellaquila, Trani (Italy). To contact the controller, use the Contact section of the Site (email/phone are revealed only after an intentional user action to reduce scraping).

2. Data we process

  • Browsing data: standard server/CDN logs that may include IP address, user agent, date/time and requested URL (processed by GitHub Pages infrastructure).
  • Consent preferences: stored locally in your browser (localStorage).
  • Contact form data: name, email and message you voluntarily submit. The Site is static: submitting the form opens your email client via mailto:; messages are not stored on a server of this Site.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Provide and secure the Site — legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Respond to enquiries you send — pre-contractual steps / legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(b)/(f)).
  • Optional analytics — consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), only if accepted.
  • Legal compliance — legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).

4. Recipients

Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts. These providers act as independent controllers or processors according to their terms. No sale of personal data occurs.

5. Transfers outside the EEA

GitHub and Google may process data in the United States. Transfers rely on appropriate safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses) as described in those providers’ documentation.

6. Retention

  • Server logs: according to the hosting provider’s retention.
  • Consent preferences: until cleared by you.
  • Emails you send: retained in the controller’s mailbox only as needed to handle the request.

7. Security measures

Contact details are obfuscated in the HTML source and decoded in-browser only after a user click. The contact form includes a honeypot field to discourage bots. HTTPS is enforced by GitHub Pages.

8. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent. You may lodge a complaint with the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

9. Cookies

Details are available in the Cookie Policy.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated periodically. The “Last updated” date at the top will change accordingly.
