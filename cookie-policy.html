Cookie Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how the personal portfolio of Francesco Paolo Dellaquila (“we”, “site”) uses cookies and similar technologies, in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the ePrivacy Directive.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They can be strictly necessary for the site to function, or optional for analytics and preferences.

2. Who is the controller?

Controller: Francesco Paolo Dellaquila, Italy. Contact: use the reveal controls or contact form on the Contact page.

3. Cookies we use

Essential cookies

Required for core functionality and to remember your cookie choices. These do not require consent under GDPR/ePrivacy when strictly necessary.

  • fpd_cookie_consent_v1 (localStorage) — stores your consent preferences (essential / analytics).

Analytics cookies (optional)

Used only if you accept analytics. They help understand anonymous traffic and improve content. No advertising profiling is performed on this site today; if analytics tools are enabled later, they will load only after consent.

4. Legal basis

  • Essential cookies: legitimate interest / necessity for providing the service requested (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR / ePrivacy exemption).
  • Analytics cookies: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

5. Managing your choices

On your first visit you can Accept all, Reject optional, or open Preferences. You may clear site data in your browser at any time to reset consent and see the banner again.

6. Retention

Consent preferences are stored until you clear them or update them. Analytics data retention, if enabled, will follow the provider’s configuration and will not exceed 14 months by default.

7. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing. You may lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante Privacy).
